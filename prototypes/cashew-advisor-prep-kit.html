Before you pitch it as "nobody's solving this"

Reality check

Don't open with "no one's building AI advisors for farmers" — a judge who's seen this space will hear it as under-researched. Digital Green's FarmerChat already does this at real scale (voice + text advice over WhatsApp/Telegram, cost per farmer down from $35 to under $1), and the academic literature on it is public. That category exists and is well-funded.

What doesn't exist is a Khmer-first, M23-specific version with a real distribution channel in Cambodia. That's the actual pitch — narrower, truer, and harder for a judge to poke a hole in.

A Khmer-language AI advisor that walks M23 cashew smallholders through their exact planting-to-harvest calendar — verified with Angkor Green's agronomists, distributed through the input shops farmers already trust.

Note: "Claim Your Profile" is on tomorrow's agenda at 9:15 — have a project name picked before then.

Lean Canvas — draft to bring into the 10:40 worksheet

Problem

  • No trustworthy, real-time M23-specific guidance — farmers rely on neighbors, retailer guesswork, or nothing
  • Existing digital tools are generic: multi-crop, not Khmer-first, not M23-tuned
  • Price/market information asymmetry at harvest and sale time

Existing alternatives

  • Asking neighbors or the input shop clerk
  • Trial and error, last year's routine
  • Non-Khmer YouTube content

Solution

  • Khmer text + voice chatbot covering the full M23 lifecycle: land prep, planting, fertilizer/pest schedule, pruning, harvest, post-harvest
  • Content curated with Angkor Green

Key metrics

  • Weekly active farmers
  • % answered vs. "don't know"
  • Retailer referrals into the bot

Unique value proposition

Your cashew season, explained in Khmer, one variety at a time — not a generic crop bot translated after the fact.

Unfair advantage

  • Real relationship with Angkor Green
  • Existing retail customer base as day-one distribution
  • Own agribusiness/farm background

Channels

  • Input retail shops as referral points
  • Telegram / Messenger — no new app to install

Customer segments

  • Smallholder M23 cashew farmers in Cambodia

Early adopters

  • Existing customers of his own agri-input shop

Cost structure

  • LLM/API usage, content curation time, bot hosting, partner relationship upkeep with Angkor Green

Revenue streams

  • Freemium bot → premium bundled with input purchases; sponsorship from ag-input companies; longer-term, anonymized demand insight for suppliers

Build plan — Claude Pro + Lovable

  1. Before / during orientation — Sun 30 Aug, morning

    Walk in refined, not blank

    • Bring this canvas into the 10:40–12:00 worksheet + mini pitch block
    • Pick a project name before "Claim Your Profile" at 9:15
    • Have the one-line pitch memorized for the mini pitch challenge
  2. Knowledge base — Claude Pro, before the demo build

    Compile the M23 lifecycle

    • In this Claude project, log each stage — nursery/planting, vegetative growth, flowering, fruiting, harvest, post-harvest — with common issues and fertilizer/pest timing for each
    • Draft it in Khmer directly; Claude can check phrasing, but verify agronomy accuracy against real M23 practice, ideally with Angkor Green before the demo locks
    • Output a structured stage-by-stage doc — this becomes the chatbot's grounding content
  3. Build — Lovable, afternoon session 13:00–16:00

    Build the narrowest version that works

    • Describe a Khmer chat interface to Lovable that answers M23 questions using the knowledge base as grounding content
    • One crop, one variety, one language — the FarmerChat research above shows even well-funded teams do best scoped tight
    • Two screens is enough: the chat, and a simple visual growing-stage reference
  4. Before judging

    Pressure-test it

    • Run 8–10 realistic farmer questions in Khmer, check answers against real practice, note where it goes vague
    • Record a backup demo video/screenshots in case live wifi fails
    • Rehearse tying the demo back to the retail channel + Angkor Green relationship as the "why us"
  5. After Build Week — back in this chat

    Turn it into the pitch deck

    • Problem (cite the stats above), why now (export boom + National Cashew Policy), solution (demo), traction/why me (retail channel, Angkor Green, Finclusion 3rd place), the ask
